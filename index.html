Michael McCamy

I'm Michael McCamy, a computer science student at the University of Florida, graduating in May 2027. I went into computer science because I have always loved technology and math, and computer science is the application of those areas to solve real world issues. My favorite part of software development is building something, putting it in front of someone, and finding out quickly whether it solved their problem. Outside of coursework I spent two years in the UF Space Systems and Design Club, writing flight software for a miniature satellite and building the interface our team used to track telemetry during launch and descent.

This summer I interned at Fuzzy AI in Singapore, a small B2B startup building software that automates LinkedIn outreach. I shipped over 100 bug fixes and features, reviewed other interns' pull requests, and sat in on calls with potential customers. After graduation, I hope to either continue working in the startup sphere or work for a larger company where I can build software at scale.
