The assignment requires at least three skills, at least one of which is a transferable / soft skill. Two should come from your Skills Reflection assignment — pull those in and reuse the evidence. The third block below is already set up as the soft skill.

How it developed

Evidence

Skill three

Transferable

The soft skill — required. Cross-cultural communication, asking for help early, giving and taking code review, managing ambiguity, working across time zones

Soft skills get vague fast. Fix that by defining what the skill looks like in practice before you claim it — "asking for help early" means something you can point at; "communication" doesn't.

How I used it

One story, told properly. A moment where a misunderstanding cost you something and you changed your approach is far more convincing than a general claim about adapting well.

How it developed

What you do differently now.

Evidence

Harder to link, so describe it well: a specific meeting, a piece of feedback you got, a process you changed. If you have written feedback from a supervisor and permission to quote it, that's the strongest evidence available.
